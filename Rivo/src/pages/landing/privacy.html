Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Rivo is built on the belief that your conversations are yours alone. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and how we protect it — in plain language, not legalese.

What we collect

  • Account information you provide — name, email address, and username — to create and secure your account.
  • Messages are encrypted with per-message keys before storage. We do not have access to readable message content.
  • Basic usage diagnostics (error reports, performance metrics) to improve reliability. These are never tied to message content.
  • Push notification tokens, stored solely to deliver message notifications to your device.

How we use data

We use account data to authenticate you, deliver push notifications, and maintain account security. Diagnostic data helps us fix bugs and improve performance. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except where required by law.

Security

Every message is encrypted with a unique key before it reaches our servers (envelope encryption: per-message DEKs wrapped by a KEK held in a secure vault). Server-side protections include JWT authentication with HttpOnly cookies, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and bcrypt password hashing. Keys are never stored alongside message data.

Your choices

You control your privacy settings directly in the app — including online presence visibility, profile picture visibility, and email discoverability. To request data export or account deletion, contact us at privacy@rivo.ir.
